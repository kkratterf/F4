There's a sticker for that

F4 comes with stickers ready to use. Add your own and every one is a search away.

Paste one from WhatsApp or Telegram, drop files or click Add. Right-click a sticker to move it into a folder.

Drop a .wastickers pack on the panel or paste it with ⌘V: F4 unpacks it into its own folder.

Animated emoji and F4's own stickers come built in, so the panel is never empty.

A sticker from WhatsApp or Telegram: right-click it, Copy, then ⌘V in F4. ⌘D keeps one in Favourites.
